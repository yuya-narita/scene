# AHAKO EXPRESSIVE TEXT EFFECTS V2.24

- Added `DUST / 塵になって風に流される` to Scene disappear options.
- The text erodes from right to left in narrow bands instead of breaking all at once.
- Fine particles appear only along the moving erosion edge and drift with the wind.
- Temporary strips and particles are created only when disappearance starts, then removed.
- Added `dust` to Scene Format v1 schema and Studio UI.
- Audio, ending audio asset handling, cover viewport, and public-player files are unchanged.
