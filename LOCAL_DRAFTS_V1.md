# Local Drafts v1 — v0.2.32

- IndexedDB local drafts, maximum 10.
- Debounced autosave while editing Easy/Advanced.
- Saves title/body, Scene document, selected Scene, REC progress, cover and registered local asset Blobs.
- Startup shows the most recently edited local draft card.
- Draft manager lists all local drafts with Scene count, REC progress and last-edited time.
- Continue / delete / create-new controls.
- AUTO REC saves completed Scene timing at every boundary and can resume from the next Scene.
- `.scene` remains the portable/final backup format; local drafts are working state.
