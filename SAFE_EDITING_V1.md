# Safe Editing v0.2.29

Adds:
- Confirmation before Sample replaces a non-empty title/body.
- One-step Studio Undo for:
  - Sample replacement
  - Scene deletion
  - Scene addition
  - Scene merge
  - Scene split
  - Scene reorder
- Normal text-entry undo remains browser-native.
- Undo appears as a bottom action bar and restores the immediately previous structural state.
